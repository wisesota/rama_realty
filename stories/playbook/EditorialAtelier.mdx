# Rama Realty: The Editorial Property Atelier

Rama Realty replaces standard SaaS conventions with an **Editorial Property Atelier** designed for high-net-worth real estate discovery in Dubai.

---

## 🏛️ Key Design & Product Principles

### 1. Airy Gallery Canvas
- Light, breathable canvas (`#fbfbf8`) with generous whitespace.
- Restrained color application: `#82b8d7` (sky) and `#b99463` (sand) are used exclusively for accents, indicators, and highlights.
- Avoid heavy saturated primaries, generic dark neon modes, or bloated SaaS dashboards.

### 2. Architectural Media Discipline
- **16px Editorial Media Corners**: Photography is framed with 16px architectural media corners without playful rounded bubbles or decorative organic blobs.
- Media frames maintain deterministic aspect ratios (16:10, 4:3, or 1:1) and structural borders.

### 3. Compact Precision Controls
- Controls measure between 6px and 8px radii, 2.75rem (44px) touch targets for high accessibility.
- Avoid generic pill-shaped buttons, heavy drop shadows, or floating glassmorphic badges.

### 4. Single-Line Desktop Hero Rule
- The desktop hero heading resolves to **one visual line** at `1280px` and above, wrapping intentionally only below the desktop breakpoint.

### 5. Voice-First Discovery Contract
- Speech-to-speech AI discovery powered by Gemini Live.
- Interactive voice signal with deterministic state visualization (idle, listening, processing, speaking).
- Explanatory criteria chips and clear AI-to-human advisor escalation pathways.
