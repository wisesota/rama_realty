# Phase 1 operating thesis

**Status:** CTO-selected hypothesis; customer, partner, and legal validation still required before the Phase 1 gate closes.

## Narrow wedge

Start with serious cross-border buyers evaluating Dubai freehold primary/off-plan and recently completed homes from **AED 2 million to AED 15 million**. Their job is not “find listings”; it is “decide whether this project, payment schedule, delivery risk, and exit/liquidity profile fit my constraints without relying on sales pressure.” The first distribution lane is a small cohort introduced by one licensed Dubai buyer-advisory/brokerage partner, not broad paid acquisition.

The current market supports this wedge: off-plan represents most transactions while delivery schedules are uncertain and investor caution is rising. Conversational search is already available from large portals, so Rama competes on evidence, change history, and decision assurance rather than query UX alone. Market evidence and source dates are maintained in the approved CTO plan.

## Operating boundary

Rama is software decision support plus a consented introduction to a separately licensed brokerage. Rama does not claim to be a broker, appraiser, investment adviser, conveyancer, or source of guaranteed availability. A licensed human owns regulated brokerage conduct, project/listing verification, negotiation, and transaction records.

## Supply hypothesis

The preferred first supply lane is **one licensed partner's own authorized inventory and media**, reconciled against DLD/Trakheesi permit and project data. Property Finder's Enterprise API is a candidate partner integration because it is server-to-server, supports listing reads for an authorized customer, and enforces Dubai permit/license workflows; it is not evidence of a right to republish the full Property Finder marketplace. DLD open data supplies official market/project/broker context, not live unit availability.

Required contract fields before ingestion: data owner, permitted surfaces, property/project/ad/permit identifiers, publication state, source observation time, freshness SLA, attribution, media/floor-plan/document rights, geographic scope, deletion/takedown SLA, commercial terms, and audit rights. Sources: [Property Finder Enterprise API](https://api-docs.propertyfinder.net/enterprise-api/index.html), [DLD real-estate data](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/open-data/real-estate-data/), [DLD Trakheesi API registration](https://backoffice.dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/api-gateway-registration?appId=3).

## Monetization hypothesis

Pilot as partner-funded decision infrastructure: a fixed monthly workspace/data fee plus a qualified-introduction fee only where legally and contractually permitted and disclosed. Do not optimize for lead volume. A direct buyer subscription can be tested later only if buyers demonstrate willingness to pay for an evidence pack without advisor access.

## Pilot scorecard

| Measure | Pilot gate |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmed brief accuracy | at least 90% of hard constraints accepted without correction after review |
| Evidence completeness | at least 95% of displayed decision assertions have source, observed time, state, and rights metadata |
| Freshness | 100% within contracted SLA; expired records fail closed |
| Decision progression | at least 40% of qualified participants complete a comparison or scenario after discovery |
| Trust | at least 80% can explain why the lead option matched and identify one risk/caveat |
| Advisor quality | at least 90% of accepted introductions receive a licensed response within the agreed SLA |
| Economics | partner gross margin positive after data/provider and advisor handling costs in the pilot cohort |

Research target: 12-15 qualified cross-border buyers, 3-5 licensed advisors, and 2 potential supply/CRM partners. Record disconfirming evidence. No public claim is earned by the pilot until the claims registry contains the source, sample, date, reviewer, expiry, and exact approved copy.
